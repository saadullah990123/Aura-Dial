This policy explains what personal information [[LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]] ("we", "us") collects

## What we collect
When you place an order we collect your name, phone number, delivery address, city, any notes
you add, and optionally your email address, plus the items you ordered. We also record the
status of your order as it moves from placement to delivery.

You do not need an account to order, and we do not create one.

## Why we use it
We use this information only to process, deliver and support your order, to contact you about
it, and to keep records we are required or entitled to keep. [[We do not use it for marketing. DELETE OR EDIT TO MATCH FACTS.md]]

## Payment
Orders are paid in cash on delivery. We do not collect or store card or bank details on this site.

## Your shopping cart
Items in your cart are saved in your own browser so they are still there when you return.
This stays on your device until you check out or clear your browser data.

## Technical data
To protect the site from abuse, we use your connection's IP address in hashed (scrambled) form
for rate limiting, and it expires automatically. We do not use analytics, advertising or
tracking tools on this site.

## Who handles your data
- Our database provider, Neon, stores order records on our behalf.
- Our image provider, Cloudinary, serves the product pictures, so your browser requests them from Cloudinary.
- Our hosting provider, [[HOSTING PROVIDER]], runs the website.
- Delivery couriers, [[COURIER NAMES]], receive your name, phone number and address to deliver your order.
- If you tap "Message us", you are taken to WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy.
We do not sell your personal information.
