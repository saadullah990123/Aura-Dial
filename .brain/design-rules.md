# UI & Design Guidelines

## Brand Theme
- Primary Colors: Dark Luxury Black/Charcoal background with Gold accents (#D4AF37 / #C5A059).
- Typography: Clean serif and sans-serif pairings for luxury aesthetic.

## Navigation & Footer
- Header navigation contains: Home, Watches, Glasses, About, Contact, and Track Order.
- Footer contains social icons: TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook.
